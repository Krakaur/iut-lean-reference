# Roadmap toward the IUT III 3.11--3.12 challenge

Status: `ROADMAP_ONLY / NOT_FORMALIZED`

Primary future source: `SRC-OFFICIAL-9A7EE3C77B1C7717`, *Inter-universal
Teichmüller Theory III: Canonical Splittings of the Log-theta-lattice*.

T01--T08 do not prove, approximate, or validate the transition from 3.11 to
3.12. Their relevance is methodological: they demonstrate how a difficult
source should be decomposed into typed claims, localizers, rival readings,
executable examples and independent verification gates.

## Required information layers before formal work

1. A page- and clause-level map of Theorem 3.11, Corollary 3.12, every imported
   definition, and every antecedent result actually used in the transition.
2. A notation graph that distinguishes arithmetic objects, log-shells,
   theta-related objects, hulls, indeterminacies and the morphisms between them.
3. A dependency graph that marks whether each edge is definitional, proved,
   cited, reconstructed, functorial, or merely explanatory.
4. A ledger of quantifiers and ambient categories. Similar notation in
   different Hodge theaters or universes must not become definitional equality.
5. A catalogue of normalization conventions and inequalities, including which
   operations preserve or weaken them.
6. Small models and mutation tests for every abstraction that admits them.
7. A prior-art map covering existing Lean prototypes, critiques, expository
   treatments and formal libraries, without transferring their trust status.

## Proposed formalization sequence

The next useful milestones are not “formalize 3.12.” They are smaller contracts:

- reconstruct the exact input and output types of 3.11;
- represent each indeterminacy as typed data rather than prose;
- formalize the relevant hull/closure operations independently;
- isolate scalar, order and volume inequalities from geometric provenance;
- prove transport lemmas only after their domains and comparison maps are fixed;
- build countermodels for illicit identification of theaters or coordinates;
- state a dependency-complete 3.11 interface without assuming the 3.12 conclusion;
- only then contract the 3.11-to-3.12 inference for independent semantic review.

## AI-oriented corpus requirements

For each future claim, retain `source_id`, printed page, clause, exact role,
epistemic status, typed dependencies, competing interpretations, discriminator,
Lean declaration if any, axiom surface, and gate status. Training examples
should include rejected mutations and explanations of why they fail. Positive
examples alone encourage models to reproduce notation without learning the
boundary between formal algebra and geometric provenance.

## Stop conditions

Formalization must stop rather than fill gaps when source bytes are unavailable,
two scientifically different interpretations remain compatible with the
evidence, a hard geometric result appears only as an input field, or a proof
would require identifying objects that the source relates only through a map or
subquotient. These are `NO_GO` conditions, not inconveniences to hide with
axioms.
