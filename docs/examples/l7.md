## Quotient counts

The nonzero sign pairs are `{1,6}`, `{2,5}` and `{3,4}`, so the multiplicative

## Signed-affine test

Let `g=(2,true)` and `h=(5,false)`. The semidirect law gives

```text
g*h = (2-5, true) = (4,true) mod 7.
```

A direct product would have translation `2+5=0`, so the closed theorem
`Tutorial.T06_example_l7_semidirect` proves the correct product and rejects
zero.

For `z=6`, applying `h` first yields four; applying `g` next yields
`-4+2=5`. The product `(4,true)` also sends six to `-6+4=5`, which exercises
the T07 convention independently of the five-element example.

The full signed-affine group has fourteen elements, while the additive quotient
has four. Together with the multiplicative quotient size three, these facts are
compiled as the typed T11 vector `(3,4,14)` in `Tutorial.lean`.
