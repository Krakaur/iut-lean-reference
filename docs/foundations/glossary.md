# Glossary and type discipline

## `Fl l`

The coordinate ring `ZMod l`. Under the unit's prime hypothesis it is the
finite field with `l` elements. The notation is a coordinate model, not a
geometric cusp set.

## Sign subgroup

The subgroup of `(ZMod l)ˣ` generated by `-1`. For prime `l >= 5` it contains
exactly `1` and `-1` and has order two.

## `MultiplicativeLabelGroup l`

The quotient of nonzero coordinates by the sign subgroup. It has `(l-1)/2`
elements. The name deliberately says “group” rather than identifying it with a
geometric set of labels.

## `AdditiveSignOrbit l`

The quotient of all coordinates by `x ~ -x`. It includes the fixed class of
zero, so it has `(l+1)/2` classes. It is not the multiplicative quotient and is
not the signed-affine group.

## `SignedLabelCarrier l`

An abstract carrier equipped with a nonempty distinguished orbit of charts to
`Fl l`. Only two representatives are admitted relative to a chosen chart: the
chart itself and its postcomposition with negation. This models a coordinatized
consequence of Definition 6.1(i); it is not asserted to be a geometric cusp
object.

## `SignedAffine l`

A visible pair consisting of a translation in `Fl l` and a Boolean sign. It is
a group of `2*l` transformations. Its action on `Fl l` is
`z ↦ sign*z + translation`.

## Ordinary torsor versus the source's affine-chart structure

An ordinary torsor under a group has a free transitive group action. The
Definition 6.1(i) structure used here is represented through an orbit of
coordinate charts. A carrier with `l` points cannot be a free transitive torsor
under a `2*l` element group. These types must not be merged.

## Poly-action

The source's poly-actions involve richer families of morphisms and geometric
provenance. None is constructed in T01--T11. Replacing “poly-action” by an
ordinary Lean `MulAction` would be an unsupported semantic collapse.

## `PolyMorphism X Y`

The algebraic prototype represents a poly-morphism as a set of functions
`X → Y`. The Boolean example retains both identity and negation, and
`IUT1.PolyMorphism.PA01_bool_two_map_ne_singletons` proves that the collection
is not either singleton. This type models the collection-valued interface only;
it is not a source category of geometric morphisms.

## `AlgebraicPolyActionPrototype H G`

A surjective homomorphism `H →* G` generates, for every `g : G`, the collection
of left translations by the complete fiber over `g`. Exact composition is
proved by `IUT1.AlgebraicPolyActionPrototype.PA04_arrows_mul`. In the synthetic
`l=5` example, two translations lie over the identity quotient label. The
structure contains no capsule, bridge, Hodge theater or source provenance, so
its name and epistemic status always retain the qualifier “algebraic
prototype.”

## Epistemic statuses

- `SOURCE_EXPLICIT`: the cited source passage states the relevant definition.
- `DERIVED`: the statement is a mathematical consequence of source data.
- `DERIVED_COORDINATIZED_MODEL`: the statement concerns an explicit model that
  preserves a source role without claiming to realize the full geometric object.
- `DISCRIMINANT_COUNTERMODEL`: a project-created negative test, not a theorem
  attributed to the source.
- `PRIOR_ART_ESTABLISHED`: earlier mathematical or Lean content was found.
- `NOT_FOUND_WITHIN_AUDITED_SCOPE`: a bounded negative search, never proof of
  global novelty.
