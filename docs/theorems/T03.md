## Plain mathematical translation

The units `1` and `-1` form the two-element sign subgroup. T03 models it as the
cyclic subgroup generated by `-1` inside the units of `ZMod l` and proves that
its cardinality is two.

## Lean statement

```lean
theorem T03_C02_card_SignSubgroup
    (hl5 : 5 ≤ l) : Nat.card (SignSubgroup l) = 2
```

The proof reduces subgroup cardinality to the order of `-1` and checks that
`l ≠ 2`. This is a derived algebraic obligation, not a cardinal supplied as a
structure field.

## Example

`Tutorial.T03_example_l5` proves that the sign subgroup in `(ZMod 5)ˣ` has two
elements. Its elements are represented by one and four.

## Discriminator

At characteristic two the two signs coincide and the subgroup has one element.
The explicit example and hypothesis prevent an implementation from treating
`{±1}` as automatically two-element in every ring.

## Does not establish

T03 does not say that every two-element subgroup is the relevant source sign
subgroup, nor does it construct an action on geometric labels.
