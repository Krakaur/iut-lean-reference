## Plain mathematical translation

There are `l` choices for the translation and two choices for the sign, hence
the signed-affine group has `2*l` elements.

## Lean statement

```lean
theorem T08_C06_card_signedAffine :
    Fintype.card (SignedAffine l) = 2 * l
```

The proof constructs an explicit equivalence from the custom structure to
`Fl l × Bool`, uses product cardinality and substitutes T01. Cardinality is not
stored in the group structure or assumed as a hypothesis.

## Closed example

`T08_example_l5_card` proves the value ten. The separate audit proves
`10 ≠ lPlusMinus 5` and computes `lPlusMinus 5=3` without axioms.

## Discriminator

The signed-affine group must not be identified with the additive quotient by
sign. At `l=5` their sizes are ten and three. This numerical gap catches a type
collapse that similar notation might otherwise hide.

## Priority and limits

T08 has direct IUT-oriented Lean prior art: Takkun's repository models the
symmetry by `DihedralGroup l` and proves `2*l`. T08 is therefore a traceability
and regression result, not a novelty claim. It does not prove the later
dihedral isomorphism for this custom carrier or any geometric realization.
