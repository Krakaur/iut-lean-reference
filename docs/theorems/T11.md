## Plain mathematical translation

For modulus five, the multiplicative label quotient has two elements, the
additive quotient by sign has three classes, and the signed-affine group has ten
elements. For modulus seven the corresponding values are three, four and
fourteen.

```text
l=5: (2, 3, 10)
l=7: (3, 4, 14)
```

## Why the vector is typed

The three entries do not describe three formulas for one object. They belong to
three different types:

1. units modulo the sign subgroup;
2. all additive coordinates modulo negation;
3. the group of signed-affine transformations.

T11 keeps the types present in its Lean statement. This makes the examples
usable as regression fixtures for a parser or model that might otherwise merge
similar notation.

## Lean statement shape

```lean
theorem T11_l5_cardinality_vector :
    Nat.card (MultiplicativeLabelGroup 5) = 2 ∧

```

The `l=7` declaration has the analogous values. Both reuse general theorems;
they are not proofs by finite enumeration.
