## Plain mathematical translation

The multiplication created in T06 must represent composition of the displayed
affine functions. If `g*h` means “first `h`, then `g`,” applying the product to
`z` must equal applying `h` and then `g`.

## Lean statement

```lean
theorem T07_C06_act_mul (g h : SignedAffine l) (z : Fl l) :
    (g * h).act z = g.act (h.act z)
```

The proof unfolds the T06 multiplication and checks the four possible pairs of
sign bits. It does not introduce a second action or derive compatibility from a
prepackaged dihedral model.

## Closed example

For `g=(1,true)`, `h=(2,false)` and `z=3` modulo five, `h` first sends three to
zero, and `g` sends zero to one. Both sides of T07 therefore evaluate to one.

## Discriminator

Reversing the multiplication convention produces `h*g=(3,true)` in this
example and a different action value. Placing the translation inside the sign,
as `sign*(z+a)`, also fails the closed test.

## Does not establish

T07 is an action on the coordinate field. It does not construct an action on a
geometric label set or justify replacing a source poly-action with an ordinary
group action.
