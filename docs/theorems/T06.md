## Plain mathematical translation

A signed-affine transformation is represented by a translation `a` and a sign
`s`. It sends `z` to `±z+a`. Composing `(b,t)` first and `(a,s)` second gives
translation `a + s*b` and sign `s*t`.

## Lean representation

```lean
structure SignedAffine (l : ℕ) where
  translation : Fl l
  negative : Bool

(a,s) * (b,t) = (a + signAct s b, Bool.xor s t)
```

The project proves associativity, identity and inverse directly by cases on the
sign bits. `SignedAffine` is not an abbreviation for `DihedralGroup`, so the
coordinate convention remains inspectable.

## Closed example

Over `ZMod 5`, let `g=(1,true)` and `h=(2,false)`. T06 proves
`g*h=(4,true)` and also proves that the translation is not three.

## Discriminator

A direct-product mutation would return translation `1+2=3`. The negative sign
must act on the second translation, so the correct value is `1-2=4`.

## Priority and limits

The group construction is standard mathematics, and Mathlib has generic
semidirect products. The contribution is the visible, source-audited convention
and its mutation test. T06 does not identify the group with geometric
automorphisms or construct the source torsor.
