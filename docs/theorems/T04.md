## Plain mathematical translation

There are `l-1` nonzero elements in the prime field. Quotienting by the
two-element sign subgroup pairs `x` with `-x`, giving `(l-1)/2` classes.

## Lean statement

```lean
theorem T04_C02_C04_card_MultiplicativeLabelGroup
    (hl5 : 5 ≤ l) :
    Nat.card (MultiplicativeLabelGroup l) = lStar l
```

Here `lStar l = (l-1)/2`. The proof computes the unit group, applies the
subgroup/quotient cardinal identity, substitutes T03 and solves the arithmetic.

## Example

Modulo five, the two classes are `{1,4}` and `{2,3}`. The executable theorem
`Tutorial.T04_example_l5` returns cardinality two.

## Discriminator

Zero is not a unit and therefore does not appear in this quotient. Adding zero
would produce the T05 additive quotient with three rather than two classes at
`l=5`.

## Does not establish

The project name `MultiplicativeLabelGroup` avoids equating this quotient group
definitionally with a geometric `LabCusp`. Such an identification would require
additional source data and a proved map.
