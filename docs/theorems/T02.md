## Plain mathematical translation

Over a field of odd characteristic, the equation `-x=x` implies `2x=0`. Since
two is nonzero, `x=0`. Thus zero is the only coordinate fixed by the sign
involution.

## Lean statement

```lean
theorem T02_C01_C05_neg_eq_self_iff
    (hl5 : 5 ≤ l) (x : Fl l) : -x = x ↔ x = 0
```

The proof explicitly derives that `2` is nonzero modulo `l`; it does not hide
oddness in an undocumented global instance.

## Example and failure mode

`Tutorial.T02_example_l5` specializes the equivalence to `ZMod 5`. Modulus two
is the negative test: there `1=-1`, so a nonzero fixed point exists. This shows
why silently dropping the odd-prime context changes the statement.

## Discriminator

T02 separates sign orbits into one fixed zero class and two-element nonzero
classes. That distinction is later responsible for `(l+1)/2` additive classes,
as opposed to `(l-1)/2` multiplicative classes.

## Does not establish

The theorem concerns coordinate negation only. It does not construct the
source's abstract chart orbit or a geometric involution.
