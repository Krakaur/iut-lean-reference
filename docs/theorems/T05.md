## Plain mathematical translation

The source does not merely choose one bijection from an abstract carrier to
`F_l`. It specifies an orbit of allowed charts, with the two representatives
related by postcomposition with negation. T05 records what survives after
quotienting both the carrier and `F_l` by sign.

## Four obligations

`T05a` proves that a sign-equivariant equivalence sends equivalent points to
equivalent points and therefore descends to an equivalence of quotient types.

`T05b` proves that replacing an allowed chart by its post-negation gives the
same transported involution and exactly the same descended map. This is an
equality of functions, stronger than equal cardinalities.

`T05c` is a project-created mutation test. On the definitionally distinct
carrier `ULift (Fl 5)`, multiplication by two is sign-equivariant, but it is not
in the two-chart source orbit and it induces a different quotient map. Thus
“all sign-equivariant charts” is too broad.

`T05d` constructs canonical representatives and proves that the additive sign
quotient has `lPlusMinus l = (l+1)/2` classes. It transports that count to every
contracted `SignedLabelCarrier` only after constructing a quotient equivalence.

## Example

Modulo five the quotient classes are `{0}`, `{1,4}` and `{2,3}`.
`Tutorial.T05_example_l5_additive_sign_orbits` proves the cardinality is three.

## Discriminator

The key discriminator is map independence under the two authorized chart
representatives. A model that stores only cardinality, or replaces the
distinguished orbit with every equivariant chart, fails T05b or T05c.

## Does not establish

`SignedLabelCarrier` is a coordinatized interface. T05 does not construct the
geometric carrier, its cusps, a poly-action, or an ordinary torsor under the
`2*l` signed-affine group.
